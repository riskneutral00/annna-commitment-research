# annnä Marketplace — INTERFACES (the seams)

*Four seams: **downward** to the closed service (the only place infrastructure lives), **upward** to the app (which renders the store), **across** to the harness (which installs cross it as ordinary elicitation), and **down** to the engine (which is not marketplace-aware). The governing constraint, **scoped** *(2026-08-29 — read as absolute it was already false, and visibly so: the downward list below has grown three verbs since this line was written)*: **zero new seam verbs on the inward seams** — app, harness, engine — where the marketplace rides existing shapes or it has gone wrong. The **downward** seam to the closed service has a **ruled growth path** instead: its verb list grows by ruling and only by ruling, each addition recorded with the section that needed it — `entitlements.grant` and `catalog.publish`/`catalog.unpublish` each arrived exactly that way.*

---

## §1. To the closed service (downward)

The store's infrastructure — catalog, curation, payments, entitlements, licensed-asset delivery — is a **closed hosted service in a separate private repo**, reached over a **versioned, authenticated API**. Named here by shape only:

- `catalog.list(good, filters) → catalog documents` — browse (good = skin | template; filters = category/tags/featured).
- `catalog.get(id) → document` — one item, full document.
- **Owner-turn request shape:** the existing owner-input path may carry an internal, typed `CatalogRequest = {good: template, filters: {category?, tags?}}` to the marketplace adapter. The adapter's package-owned policy extractor derives those closed filters from the newly expressed owner need; the app integrates the call but owns no filter, ranking or continuation policy. This is not a new `AppSeam` member or public verb. `catalog.list` runs before `normalize` context assembly; `catalog.get` runs only for a chosen supplied eligible candidate.
- `entitlements.list(account) → entitlement set` — what this account owns.
- `entitlements.grant(account, item) → entitlement` — **the install's grant** *(verb added 2026-08-21 — `SPEC.md §3` said "install = entitlement grant" while this list held nothing that grants one, so BUILD Step 2's end-to-end gate could only run against a pre-seeded map)*. Alpha: granted directly (templates are free listings until the transaction surface exists; skins stay admin-curated); post-alpha: `transaction.complete` is what causes the service to grant, and this verb is the shape the grant arrives through either way.
- `assets.sign(pack, account) → short-lived URLs` — licensed derivatives, entitled accounts only.
- `catalog.publish(document) / catalog.unpublish(id)` — **templates: credentialed to the publishing owner's session** (FD-82); **skins: admin identity through the pack pipeline**. **`catalog.unpublish` is bound to the listing's own publisher** *(2026-08-29 — the pair was credentialed for *publish* and said nothing about who may unpublish, so any signed-in owner credential read as sufficient to take down anyone's listing)*: only the account that published a listing may unpublish it, and any other caller is refused; the skin path stays admin identity. *(Verbs added 2026-08-21 — §2 ends "publish to the catalog" and FR38 requires an app-authored bundle to reach it, while no listed call could put anything in or take anything out; `../security/SPEC.md §11`'s two-paths-per-good and marketplace P2/P4 govern who can reach these and what may travel.)*
- `transaction.begin / transaction.complete` — **post-alpha**; the only calls whose substance is the closed service's own, and they live entirely behind this seam. The open half never sees their contents — it observes the resulting entitlement state and nothing else (`SPEC.md §5`).

**What a read answers with, by good and by standing** *(2026-09-12 — `catalog.get` said "one item, full document" beside a `SPEC.md §4` law forbidding any licensed rendition from reaching an unentitled account, and the two read together as either a licence to leak or a contradiction; neither is the law, and the variants below are what §4 and E1/E4 already require, printed so a builder does not have to derive them)*. **This adds no authorization policy**: who is entitled to what stays `SPEC.md §4`'s, the withdrawal rule stays `SPEC.md §5`'s, and the entitlement decision stays the closed service's. These are the **shapes** the answers take:

- **Skin, unentitled** — `catalog.list` and `catalog.get` carry the pack's catalog metadata, its `palette` and its `preview` (`SPEC.md §1.1`) and **no `derivatives` member in any form**: no rendition URL signed or unsigned, no LQIP, nothing inlined as data. The palette-only preview is fully served by this variant, which is why E1 asserts it works for both accounts.
- **Skin, entitled** — the same document, plus `derivatives` delivered as `assets.sign`'s **short-lived signed URLs**, minted per account: they expire, and one account's URL does not serve another's request (E1/E4).
- **Template, listed** — `catalog.get` returns the **whole `SPEC.md §1.2` bundle**, and "full document" is exact here rather than loose: the bundle is data-free by construction (`SCENARIOS.md` F3), so it carries no licensed or private member for an entitlement to gate.
- **Template, withdrawn standing** — for that account and that item, `catalog.get` and `entitlements.grant` are **both refused**, and the refusal carries **no reason** (`SPEC.md §5` — there is no field for one). This is `SPEC.md §4`'s "re-fetch and re-install standing" ending, stated at the two calls that would otherwise reach around it; the installed fork is untouched, and the same account installing a **different** eligible item, or a different account installing this one, is unaffected.

An unentitled response is the **absence** of the licensed members, never a placeholder that names them — a redacted field is still a disclosure, and E4 is content-based.

**CI discipline (carried): mocked always.** No closed-service call ever runs in CI. This repo stubs the **whole service**: a canned catalog (the shipped four skins, **one store-skin fixture**, and the two seed templates — **throwaway fixtures**, FR38: mock content, never a shipped default), an entitlement map that covers the fixture for one account and not another, and a **test signer that mints genuinely expiring URLs** — never a dead stub, because the signed-URL laws (E1/E4) must be *executable* against the mock, not vacuously green. **The transaction stubs are INERT** *(2026-08-29 — `transaction.begin`/`transaction.complete` are marked post-alpha here while `BUILD.md` Step 1 gates E7 on exercising "the store's entitlement and transaction surface"; with nothing standing behind the verbs, E7 was unbuildable at the step that owns it)*: the mock stubs both verbs **inert** — each accepts the call and records that it was made, and **causes nothing**: no entitlement, no ledger record, no state change anywhere. That is precisely what makes E7 executable, and what makes its assertion an absence rather than a balance. **The mock is the contract test** — the service must honor these shapes, versioned; a shape change is a version, never a silent break. **That the real service honours them is proved by `BUILD.md` Step 5's separately authorized receipt** *(2026-09-13)*, which names the service and its contract version; a green mock run is a green mock run and never that evidence.

## §2. To the app (upward)

- The app renders the storefront surfaces — the gallery riser's store shelf, the browse riser, the template preview — **from catalog documents**, as `../app/SPEC.md §7`-class app-only views. It runs the preview projections and nothing else: no purchase logic, no entitlement math, no price rendering beyond what the document states. **No model call crosses this seam at render time** *(2026-09-12; law at `SPEC.md §6`)*: catalog copy comes from the catalog document's stored approved per-language entries and rule lines from the typed projection snapshot §4 already describes, so the storefront request needs no model argument and returns no model output. Ordinary translation drafting is a publisher authoring-time interaction on the publisher's own side and reaches this seam only as an already-approved version inside a published document.
- **Zero new harness render verbs.** Storefront surfaces are app-only views; nothing about the store passes through `render(surface, payload)`. Private documents are produced by the existing app account-document adapter on the app’s backend (`SPEC.md §3`), with the private definition/use law at §2. Its authenticated internal save/read/revise/remove path serves console/form and headless owner inputs alike; results use the current context/draft/readback carriers. Rendering never initiates persistence, and document writes are neither `display_settings` nor a new inward seam verb.
- **The lookup result reaches the owner's turn on an existing shape** *(2026-09-12, FD-105; retrieval law `SPEC.md §6`, carrier `../harness/INTERFACES.md §2.1`, assembly `../harness/SPEC.md §8`)*. The app's marketplace adapter performs the §1 `catalog.list`/`catalog.get` call for an **owner console** turn and stamps its bounded outcome — `results` with each candidate's catalog item id, content version, resolved eligibility and shape, or `empty`, or `unavailable` — onto the **existing** `normalize` context the app already stamps `view_context` on. **This is a widening of that same contract, not a new verb in either direction:** the inward zero-new-verbs lock in the preamble holds, the harness remains the only context assembler, and the adapter decides no continuation — it reports what the service returned and **invents no listing, emptiness or availability**. The adapter resolves eligibility from `entitlements.list`; it computes no score, rank or relevance, because none exists to compute (`SCENARIOS.md` D2/D7). Free text inside a candidate shape crosses `document`-tagged and takes the harness's quarantine read before any privileged model call.
- **The app's marketplace adapter is also what calls `catalog.publish` / `catalog.unpublish`** *(2026-09-12 — §1 credentialed the pair to "the publishing owner's session" and `../security/SPEC.md §11` says the same of the identity, while no sentence in either package named **which side of this seam places the call**; the gap read as an unresolved seam ruling when everything it needed was already decided — the owner act (`SPEC.md §2`, FD-82), the authenticated service (§1) and the app's dependency on it (`../app/INTERFACES.md §2`) all exist)*. The trigger is an **explicit owner publish act** on a `../app/SPEC.md §7`-class riser form — **never a save and never a render**: save lands a private definition and its projection in the account document (`SPEC.md §§2/3`), and the storefront surfaces above stay read-only. The adapter is handed the **saved revision's data-free publication projection**, re-runs `SPEC.md §2`'s authored-set validation as that section requires, and then calls `catalog.publish` under the **owner's current session credential** — current, so a stale or absent credential refuses rather than publishing, and another owner's credential refuses too. `catalog.unpublish` travels the same path and stays **bound to the listing's own publisher** (§1). This names no new verb in either direction: the pair is already on §1's downward list and nothing is added to the inward seams the preamble locks. The harness is on neither path — *"Publish stays out of the harness entirely"* (`../harness/SPEC.md`) — and the saved bundle's store stays the app-owned account document (`SPEC.md §3`), not the catalog.
- **What the app stubs when this package is absent: nothing.** The app's shipped-four default *is* the absent state (`../app/SCENARIOS.md` S6) — the marketplace is an addition to a complete app, never a dependency of it.

## §3. To the harness (the install crossing)

- A template install reaches the harness as **ordinary elicitation input**: the bundle's shapes become a sequence of proposals through the existing propose→confirm flow. **Selection precedes that crossing, and has its own carrier** *(2026-09-12, FD-105 — this seam receives an **already chosen** bundle, which is why the lookup's result needed a producer and got the §2 stamp above rather than a new verb here)*: the owner's choose/customize/decline over the supplied eligible candidates happens on the owner turn through `../harness/INTERFACES.md §2.1`'s `catalog_result` and the ordinary owner-input path, and only a chosen candidate's `catalog.get` document arrives here to be walked. A decline reaches nothing at this seam at all. The harness's B1 scope discipline applies per proposal; B2 ask-once applies to repeated parameters across the walk.
- **Zero new tools, zero new verbs; the floor is untouched.** Installing is *authoring* — nothing outward fires without its own basis, exactly as if the owner had spoken each shape to the console. The same applies to opening a use of a saved private definition: its owner/id/revision and fresh request identity remain explicit under `SPEC.md §2`; only validated configuration enters ordinary proposals, never incidental participants or copied live authority.
- What the harness stubs here: **nothing** — this package builds after the harness is real (`BUILD.md`). The marketplace's own suite scripts the harness where a scenario needs one.

## §4. To the engine

- An installed template writes **only constructs the engine already accepts**: kinds, rules from the closed menu, Shared shapes, boards — with fork provenance recorded in history. An off-menu rule shape was already refused at the install door (`SPEC.md §3`), upstream of the engine's own refusal; the door is a courtesy, the engine is the law.
- **The engine is not marketplace-aware** — deliberately. No marketplace field, flag, or table exists in engine truth — the one ruled exception is a projection, not a field: the **candidate-shape ghost**, the engine's fourth display projection (FD-34, `../RULINGS.md` — the deliberate exception to the zero-new-seam-shapes posture), display-only and minting nothing, consumed by `SPEC.md §6`/D3. Note recorded in `SPEC.md §7`: the no-export ruling holds because a bundle is **authored as a data-free §1.2 shape, not extracted from a populated board** (FD-82 closed user supply; P4 is the extract refusal).
- The marketplace adapter requests the candidate-shape ghost through the existing harness `calculate` contract and consumes its declared projection snapshot. It adds no marketplace tool, direct engine read, or new projection; the ghost is computed on demand against the owner's allowed availability and creates no Shared row or token digest (`../engine/SPEC.md §0`/`§5`; transport at `../engine/INTERFACES.md §2a`, return contract at `../harness/INTERFACES.md §1.1`; engine S6 / marketplace D3).

## §5. Stub strategy

- **Service mock** (per §1): canned catalog + entitlement map + a **real test signer minting genuinely expiring URLs** — with **caller-identity control** *(2026-08-31, F-29)*: the mock accepts a test-selected account identity per call, so the entitled/unentitled/second-account walks (P2a/P5a/P8's shapes) drive both sides of every entitlement assertion — never a dead stub, because E1 and E4 must be *executable* against the mock rather than vacuously green (§1 carries the reasoning). The same mock serves this package's suite, the app's S6/S7, and CI everywhere. **One artifact, one first producer** *(2026-09-13)*: it is first stood up and consumed at `../app/BUILD.md` Step 7 and **completed** by `BUILD.md` Step 1's three-valued standing and printed response variants; no consumer cans a second catalog, and the ownership row is `BUILD.md` Step 0's.
- **The empty-and-unavailable catalog controls** *(2026-09-12 — `SPEC.md §6`'s four outcomes each need a cause a suite can produce, and a canned catalog that is always populated can produce only one of them)*: the mock's **catalog is suite-selectable** — populated, **genuinely empty**, or **unavailable** (the call refuses or does not complete) — and it **records that the call was made**, so D5 can assert a real lookup rather than a narrated one. An empty catalog and an unavailable service are separate selections, because the whole point of the row is that they are separate answers. Candidate withdrawal and version movement ride the suite-mutable entitlement map and the canned catalog together.
- **The entitlement-withdrawal control** *(named 2026-08-29 — E5, E6 and E9 each need a withdrawal to happen partway through a run, and no control existed for a suite to cause one)*: the mock's **entitlement map is suite-mutable** — a test grants and withdraws an entitlement between calls, and the withdrawal carries no reason across the seam, because there is no field for one (`SPEC.md §5`). That mutability is the whole mechanism those three assert against. **Standing is three-valued, not a flag** *(2026-09-12 — a map that records only whether an entitlement exists cannot tell a **withdrawn** account from one that was **never granted**, so a direct `entitlements.grant` would hand straight back what a withdrawal had just ended, and E9's denial would be unbuildable against this mock)*: the map distinguishes **never-granted · granted · withdrawn** per account and item — or any equivalent standing the mock can deny from — so `entitlements.grant` against **withdrawn** standing **refuses**, while a legitimate **first** install from never-granted standing still succeeds and a second account is unaffected. The refusal exports **no reason**, the same absence as the withdrawal itself, and nothing in this control touches an installed fork or a persisted device copy — E6's offline rule is untouched, because an unreachable service is not a withdrawal.
- **The store-skin fixture, pinned** *(2026-08-29 — §1 requires "one store-skin fixture" and never said what it is made of; a fixture cut from a licensed master would put licensed IP in a public repo, which E4 treats as an incident rather than a bug)*: it is an `../assets/make-pack.mjs` run over a **non-licensed test master** — a throwaway fixture under FR38, mock content and never a shipped default.
- **Harness:** scripted transcripts for install walks (the I-family) — real harness once built; scripted seam calls before that.
- **App:** the record-and-return spies of `../app/INTERFACES.md §4`, unchanged. Add the internal document-store double specified by `../app/INTERFACES.md §4`: account selection, revision compare, lost acknowledgement, retirement/reference races and durable readback across controller instances. This is separate from the closed-service catalog mock; both can be empty or unavailable independently. P3/P6/I1/I5 require stateful persistence controls, not a render spy’s asserted text.
