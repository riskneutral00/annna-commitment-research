# Commitment relationships

Status: parked UI/UX investigation. Owner: Matt. Recorded from the 2026-10-16 interview.

Explore Obsidian with the approach Matt called “reverse engineer anything” to see whether a relationship display can explain how commitments connect to people and other commitments.

Examples Matt supplied:

- A mother sees her children's commitments and her own responsibilities, including pickups, drop-offs and calls.
- A dive center sees the instructor, boat company and equipment company needed for a dive.
- Missing cylinders appear as a requirement that has not yet been connected to a provision.

The Board may show commitments together or in different views. The relationship display could be on the Board or within a commitment; no layout is selected. Obsidian is a reference to study, not a selected store or runtime.

Next evidence: compare these examples in a relationship view and the other proposed views. Check whether users can tell what is missing and who is responsible without reading the conversation. No prototype or exploration has run.
