what it never does

Four refusals, built in.

  • A promised time is a promise to a real person. Nothing touches another human without your yes.
  • Your life is not content. Private commitments block availability without ever being visible.
  • The math is never a guess. Every number comes from deterministic code — the AI cannot author one.
  • Emptiness is worth defending. Time you win back is yours — a freed hour is never re-listed behind your back.
