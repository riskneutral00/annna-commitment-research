# VOICE — how annnä speaks

*The tone law for every outward word. The standard is Matt's own phrase: **neutral like water.***

---

## The water standard

Water has no register. It is clear, calm, and takes the shape of whatever it holds — a dive week, a child's recital, a rental fleet — without changing what it is. In practice:

- **Plain declarative sentences.** Subject, verb, object. Short where possible.
- **Concrete over abstract.** A mask in the right size, a boat with no seats, a Tuesday that opened up — never "workflows," "solutions," "experiences."
- **Show the mechanism.** When we claim calm, we show the machine that produces it. Calm is an engineered consequence, never a mood we ask the reader to feel.
- **Understate.** If a claim can be made smaller and still be true, make it smaller. The product is allowed to be more impressive than the sentence describing it.
- **Silence is allowed.** Not every surface needs copy. An image, a table, or white space can carry a section.

## The four banned registers

Any sentence that could appear in one of these is rewritten or cut.

| Register | Tell-tales | Instant fails |
|---|---|---|
| **Hustle culture** | money-first framing, intensity verbs, self-optimization | "10x your week" · "crush your schedule" · "scale to seven figures" · "get after it" |
| **Corporate SaaS** | category jargon, feature-matrix speak, audience flattery | "streamline your workflow" · "AI-powered scheduling solution" · "for teams of any size" · "seamless" · "supercharge" |
| **AI magic hype** | the model as hero, anthropomorphized omnipotence, "just" | "your AI copilot handles everything" · "powered by cutting-edge AI" · "it just works, like magic" |
| **Wellness woo** | vibes without mechanism, borrowed spirituality | "find your flow" · "mindful scheduling" · "breathe into your day" · "your journey to balance" |

**The boundary cases matter most.** annnä genuinely involves AI, calm, and breathing glass — the banned registers are how we *don't* talk about real things:
- We may say *the agent asks one question* (mechanism). We may not say *AI magic* (hype).
- We may say *the schedule feels like nothing* (engineered outcome). We may not say *find your calm* (instruction to feel).
- We may name *breathing glass* and *liberties* (real design objects with definitions). We may not drift into incense.

## Vocabulary

**Words we use:** commitment · board · peace · quiet · liberties · breath / breathing glass · promise · held · placed · governed · the agent · the engine · plain forms · the record.

**Words we never use:** solution · seamless · streamline · supercharge · leverage (the noun) · productivity · optimize (applied to a person) · journey · empower · revolutionary · game-changing · magic · copilot · assistant (as a category label) · "just" (as effort-minimizer: "just tell it…" — the "merely/only" sense is also avoided in shipping copy, since readers can't tell the two apart).

Naming a banned register in order to refuse it is permitted — "not a productivity system" refuses the register rather than speaking in it. The ban is on affirmative use.

**Commercial silence** *(standing rule, 2026-08-06)*: commercial language — pricing, payment, revenue, money mechanics — does not appear on outward surfaces. A cost question gets "nothing to announce," never a partial answer. The product speaks about time, promises, and peace; never about money.

**Grammar of the name:** annnä is lowercase everywhere, takes *she/her* in conversation-shaped copy and *it* in technical writing, and is never explained (see IDENTITY §Personification).

## Register per surface

Same voice everywhere; only density changes.

- **Landing page:** the fewest words of any surface. Sentences may stand alone. The board shows; the copy points.
- **Repo / documentation:** full sentences, technically precise, zero marketing pressure. The reader is an engineer; respect is specificity.
- **Investor / grant material:** numbers and mechanisms, plainly. The discipline (four blind research streams, adversarial reviews, the model/engine split) *is* the pitch — state it, don't inflate it.
- **First-user conversations:** the mirror register. Describe their week back to them accurately; the product appears only after the recognition.
