**Readiness.** Ready when a v1 Situation earns a ranked on-call list. Today every v1 protagonist is the empty-ladder case, which is why `../../harness/SCENARIOS.md D15` keeps `[MUST]` as the empty-ladder park and the walk carries `[SHOULD]` (FD-59 re-provenanced that suite honestly). **Writing this beat before a Situation earns the list would be designing to the mechanism — the exact inversion the meta-principle forbids**, and it is the reason this brief is a brief and not a story.

**The debt this closes.** `../README.md`'s invented-and-justified register, entry **2**: the ladder derives from held-out E's roster and owes a v1 beat.

## World

Somewhere in a v1 owner's life there is a moment where annnä cannot proceed and must reach a human who is not the owner — or must discover that there is nobody to reach and park. Sofia has no staff. Ploy has a shop but the story never gives her a second person to escalate to. TingTing has a bench of freelancers, but those are *offers*, not an on-call ladder. **No current protagonist has earned the list**, and that fact is the finding, not the failure.

**Protagonist:** unknown, and that is the first question. **Single or multi:** unknown.

## The question that precedes every other one

**Which v1 owner genuinely has somebody to escalate to, and why?** If the honest answer is "none of them", the correct outcome of this sitting is to say so and leave `D15`'s empty-ladder park as the whole of the v1 story — not to invent a deputy for one of them so the ladder has something to walk. An invented deputy is a script, and a script cannot falsify.

## Rules and their source — to ask

- Who decides the order of the list — the owner, standing policy, or the moment?
- Is a rung's silence a failure or an answer? FR34 rules reachable means *responded within the offer's hold window*; ask whether the founder reads escalation the same way.
- What ends the walk: exhaustion, a clock, or the owner returning?

## The floor (today, without the app)

To ask: today, when this owner is unreachable and something must be decided, what actually happens? Somebody's phone rings? It waits until morning? The customer is simply let down? **The floor here is likely to be "it waits", and if it is, that is the most useful thing the sitting can establish** — the empty-ladder park would then be the honest v1 mechanism rather than a stub.

## Situations to cover — the questions, not the answers

**To elicit as must-work.**
- The moment the ladder is needed: what was annnä doing, and what stopped it?
- What the next rung sees when reached — how much of the situation travels with the ask?

**To elicit as must-be-refused.**
- What must a rung on the ladder *never* be able to do? A rung that can act as the owner has become a seat, and seats are OR-42's unruled territory — this sitting must not answer that question sideways.
- Whether the ladder may ever reach outward to the waiting customer on its own. The confirm-before-outward floor says no; ask whether the founder agrees under time pressure.
