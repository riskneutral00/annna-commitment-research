# Brief — the owner's own money and quota surface

**Readiness.** Ready when the founder opens a sitting on what the owner sees about money on their own board.

**The debt this closes.** Two owed beats in `../COVERAGE.md` — the **owner's own ledger surface** (1) and the month-boundary quota reset from the **windows, edits and rollovers** cluster (1). They sit together because both are about the owner looking at an aggregate rather than at one commitment.

**Why nothing covers it.** `Situation-A/situation-1.md` asks *"who owes me what, this month, right now."* The corpus asserts the engine's derived `owed` value (`../../engine/SCENARIOS.md K1`) and the **guest-side** money render on a manage token (`../../app/SCENARIOS.md G10`). Nothing asserts the **owner-facing** aggregate. Likewise `../../engine/SCENARIOS.md Q1` and `Q4` assert cap enforcement and cancel-restoration *inside* a window; `situation-5.md`'s *"10-hour allowance stands full again"* when the month turns is the boundary event, and it is asserted nowhere.

## World

Sofia, at the end of a month. Money in annnä is **tracked, never moved** — priced, owed, paid, held, settled, all marks, with the actual transfer happening outside. So the question is not payment; it is **sight**. What does she look at, and does looking at it require her to open twelve threads?

**Protagonist:** Sofia. **Single protagonist**, extending Situation A.

## The question that governs the sitting

**Is the owner's money view a page, or a question she asks the agent?** The corpus has a console and a board and no third thing. `situation-1.md` phrases the beat as a sentence she says out loud — which suggests a `calculate`-class read and a spoken answer, not a ledger screen. Ask her which she wants, and ask whether the answer changes at ten students versus a hundred.

## Rules and their source — to ask

- What counts as "owed" to her — invoiced, delivered, or promised? The engine derives a value; she has a word for it and they may not match.
- **The month boundary:** does the quota reset on the calendar month, on the enrolment anniversary, or on something she picks? `situation-5.md` says the student "opens her link on the 2nd and the promise is simply true", which assumes a calendar month and never says so.
- What happens to an unused allowance — gone, or carried? The story says Bobby's credit "rode onto a rebooking the same week and settled with it", which is one case and not the rule.

## The floor (today, without the app)

To ask: today, how does she know who owes her? A notebook, a bank app, memory, or the uncomfortable truth that she does not fully know? This is a floor worth being blunt about — the gap between what she can see today and what she could see is the product, and money is where it is widest.

## Situations to cover — the questions, not the answers

**To elicit as must-work.**
- The month-end look: what she asks, what comes back, and what she does next.
- The month turning while she does nothing — allowances full again, last month's ledger closed behind it, every entry settled or carried exactly as marked.

**To elicit as must-be-refused.**
- What must the aggregate never do? Money is tracked and never moved; ask what a totals view must be unable to trigger.
- What must a student never see of another student's standing.
- A total that silently disagrees with the individual marks. Ask whether she would rather see an honest "I can't total this" than a number.

## Held-out predictions to flag, never design to

1. Whether the aggregate is a **derived read with no stored copy** — the corpus's I1 discipline says one record, no mirrored copy, and a cached total is exactly a mirrored copy.
2. Whether a quota window rolling over is an *event* something must fire on, or purely a function of the clock evaluated on read. The first needs a trigger and a materialization story; the second needs neither, and the corpus's `FiringEvent` set would have to gain a member.
