## The first of the month writes itself

The month turns, and Sofia does nothing. Each enrolled student's thread opens its new month the way the last one closed: the **NT$1,000 base entry** lands as its own monthly commitment, each student's **10-hour allowance stands full again**, and last month's ledger sits closed behind it — every entry settled or carried exactly as it was marked. The student who hit the cap in week four and was told "your hours reset next month" opens her link on the 2nd and the promise is simply **true**: the picker offers slots again. Bobby's leftover credit from a cancelled lesson months back is long gone — it rode onto a rebooking the same week it was made and settled with it, the way credits do.

## A series in its twentieth week

Bobby's Wednesday 6:00 half-hour has now run **since the spring**. Nobody has re-booked it once: as the weeks roll forward the standing slot simply keeps materializing ahead on the board, each instance its own commitment when its week arrives. Somewhere around week ten Sofia raised her hourly rate; nothing about the series flinched — every instance already booked kept the terms it was made under, exactly as the rate-change law promises. *(What terms a **not-yet-materialized** future instance of a standing series takes after a mid-series rate change — the old terms the series began on, or the new published ones — is a genuine probe question this run surfaces and deliberately does not answer: the recorded law covers bookings made, and a series instance is made when it materializes.)* *(Registered 2026-08-22 as **OR-41** — `../../../engine/SPEC.md §3`, the pricing row.)*

## A full Tuesday, read calmly

Months in, Sofia's board is genuinely dense: a Tuesday holding three lessons, yoga, two meals, the cycle's quiet marks, and a pending reschedule from Millie. She opens it mid-morning between classes — and the board is **at rest**: everything present, nothing performing. The day reads at the level she set her own dial to, complete and quiet, nothing hidden and nothing shouting — a full life that *looks like a full life, held*, not an inbox screaming triage. While she's looking, Brownie books Thursday; she gets the small note a booking always writes, and when she turns to Thursday the new block is simply **there** — changed, sitting at rest inside whatever wake scope she has set; her tap wakes it, she reads it, and rest closes back over the day. Nothing woke on its own — the board just held one more true thing. The product's oldest promise is only testable now, with months of density on the board: **peace of mind is what a full board feels like, not what an empty one looks like.**

## A firing gives up honestly *(the per-firing budget park — the register's owed beat, scripted 2026-08-22)*

At three in the morning a form return lands on the tangle the board has been circling for days: Millie's pending reschedule, the quota edge it sits on, and a buffer rule the move would graze. The firing starts working it — and hits the ceiling every firing carries, the declared step-and-spend budget, before it reaches an answer. It does not keep going, and it does not error: it **parks**. Nothing was sent, nothing was half-moved, and no meter ran on while nobody watched.

Sofia meets it with her coffee: one parked card, and the card **says why** — not "something went wrong" but *this needed more work than an unattended run is allowed to spend; here is where it stopped.* She reads the tangle in one look, answers the one question the firing couldn't settle alone, and the loop finishes the job attended, in seconds, on her yes. The park cleared because she cleared it; the night spent what the ceiling permits and not one step more. The 3 a.m. loop is exactly where an unbounded run would otherwise be discovered on an invoice — this board discovered it as one quiet card instead.

## The past stays answerable

Sofia asks the agent what March made. The answer comes off the board's own record — base fees, hours, who paid late and settled in April — the number that used to live in a notes file and her head. Nothing about an old month is editable from here; it is history, readable and closed. The board she has been living on all these months turns out to be the bookkeeping she never did.

## The point

One week proves mechanisms; only months prove the *shape of ownership*. The month rolled over and the ledgers, allowances, and base fees renewed themselves off the same rules set down once in situation-1. A standing series ran twenty weeks untouched and survived a rate change by the law already on the books — and surfaced one honest open question about the terms of its unmade future. And the calm the product is named for finally met its real test: a board dense with an actual life, reading at rest, every new true thing simply present when she next looks — woken only by her own hand. Even the machine's own limit showed up as calm — a firing that ran out of budget became one quiet card with the reason on it, never a runaway meter and never a 3 a.m. error. The lock-in thesis was never "you set it up here"; it's **"your months live here."**
