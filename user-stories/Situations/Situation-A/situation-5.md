# Situation 5 — months later

*Third-person, what-happens only. **Provenance: scripted** (added 2026-08-22 from the coverage review's checklist — every other run in the corpus is one week; nothing probed a board with a history, a quota rolling over, a series deep into its life, or the promise that a full board reads calmly). Same cast, same links; the only new ingredient is **time**. Who each player is lives in `story-sofia.md`, `customer.md`, and the README cast.*

## The first of the month writes itself

The month turns, and Sofia does nothing. Each enrolled student's thread opens its new month the way the last one closed: the **NT$1,000 base entry** lands as its own monthly commitment, each student's **10-hour allowance stands full again**, and last month's ledger sits closed behind it — every entry settled or carried exactly as it was marked. The student who hit the cap in week four and was told "your hours reset next month" opens her link on the 2nd and the promise is simply **true**: the picker offers slots again. Bobby's leftover credit from a cancelled lesson months back is long gone — it rode onto a rebooking the same week it was made and settled with it, the way credits do.

## A series in its twentieth week

Bobby's Wednesday 6:00 half-hour has now run **since the spring**. Nobody has re-booked it once: as the weeks roll forward the standing slot simply keeps materializing ahead on the board, each instance its own commitment when its week arrives. Somewhere around week ten Sofia raised her hourly rate; nothing about the series flinched — every instance already booked kept the terms it was made under, exactly as the rate-change law promises. *(What terms a **not-yet-materialized** future instance of a standing series takes after a mid-series rate change — the old terms the series began on, or the new published ones — is a genuine probe question this run surfaces and deliberately does not answer: the recorded law covers bookings made, and a series instance is made when it materializes.)*

## A full Tuesday, read calmly

Months in, Sofia's board is genuinely dense: a Tuesday holding three lessons, yoga, two meals, the cycle's quiet marks, and a pending reschedule from Millie. She opens it mid-morning between classes — and the board is **at rest**: everything present, nothing performing. The day reads at the level she set her own dial to, complete and quiet, nothing hidden and nothing shouting — a full life that *looks like a full life, held*, not an inbox screaming triage. While she's looking, Brownie books Thursday; **that one card arrives to attention** — the surface wakes exactly where the change is, she reads the note, and the board settles back to rest around it. The product's oldest promise is only testable now, with months of density on the board: **peace of mind is what a full board feels like, not what an empty one looks like.**

## The past stays answerable

Sofia asks the agent what March made. The answer comes off the board's own record — base fees, hours, who paid late and settled in April — the number that used to live in a notes file and her head. Nothing about an old month is editable from here; it is history, readable and closed. The board she has been living on all these months turns out to be the bookkeeping she never did.

## The point

One week proves mechanisms; only months prove the *shape of ownership*. The month rolled over and the ledgers, allowances, and base fees renewed themselves off the same rules set down once in situation-1. A standing series ran twenty weeks untouched and survived a rate change by the law already on the books — and surfaced one honest open question about the terms of its unmade future. And the calm the product is named for finally met its real test: a board dense with an actual life, reading at rest, waking only where something true just happened. The lock-in thesis was never "you set it up here"; it's **"your months live here."**
