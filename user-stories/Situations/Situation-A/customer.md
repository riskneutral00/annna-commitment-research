# The customer

*Off-app in every version of this story — no account, no calendar, only a link. Told through **Bobby** (the recurring student), with Millie and Brownie alongside. Third-person, what-happens only. The student experiences only the outward slice of Sofia's board — when she's free to teach — and never her life behind it.*

## Who

**Bobby**, one of Sofia's English students, reachable on **WeChat**. A repeat student — by the week in this story, it's already their third session, so a lesson with him is a **standing thing**, not a one-off. Alongside him: **Millie** (Spanish, on Messenger) and **Brownie** (in-person, by text). None of them has an annnä account.

## What Bobby does

Everything through a **tokenized link**, on his phone, no account:
- **He gets his own link.** A WeChat message from Sofia — *"Hi Bobby, it's Sofia — please book your English lesson with me, tap here."* The link is **unique to him**, so whatever he books lands on Sofia's board already labelled *Bobby · English*.
- **He reads her rhythm, not her life.** The link opens a month calendar that shows, on each open day, *when* she's free — Tue–Thu afternoons, Mon/Wed evenings — with some days blacked out entirely. **Nothing of her meals, runs, yoga, or cycle shows** — only teachable time.
- **He books inside her rules.** He taps Monday the 13th, its 6–9pm block opens, and he picks a start of 6:00. The end only offers **6:30 onward** — 30 minutes is Sofia's minimum, so he can't grab five. He takes 6:00–6:30, one-time, online, submits. A confirmation with the video link lands back in WeChat.
- **He sees the price, and his month.** The page shows Sofia's terms plainly before he books — **NT$1,000 a month plus NT$100 an hour** — and his own running month: hours used of his ten, and what he owes so far. He pays her the way he always has (a transfer); the app never asks him for money, it just keeps the count they'd otherwise both be keeping in their heads.

## The others, same shape
- **Millie** books off her Messenger link into the Thursday afternoon block; she tries 3:00 but it was **just taken**, so the page shows what's still open and she takes 4:00–6:00 (the max Sofia allows). Later she **moves her own lesson herself** from her link — Sofia is **not asked to approve it**; it's Millie's slot to give up, not Sofia's to okay.
- **Brownie** books in-person by his text link.

## Bobby's thread — the standing lesson

When Sofia has to cancel their Monday, Bobby gets the cancellation in WeChat **with an invitation to rebook** — the agent won't quietly drop a real person; and Sofia had to say yes before it messaged him at all. Bobby taps the same calendar, picks Wednesday the 15th, takes 6:00–6:30 online again — and this time makes it **recurring**: every Wednesday, the same half-hour, until either of them ends the series. His confirmation lands in WeChat. On Sofia's board the standing slot simply appears, week after week.

**What a student must never see:** Sofia's meals, runs, yoga, cycle — anything of her life behind the free/busy edges; or *why* a day is blacked out. **What a student must always get honestly:** only genuinely-free time offered, the price before they book and their own month's running count (hours used of ten, what they owe), a clear "that time was just taken" when they lose a race, their own power to move their own slot, and — for a standing student — a real cancellation that comes with a way to rebook.

*The students each work the same way — off-app, one unique link apiece, in the app they already use. See the cast in the README.*
