# Sofia — the protagonist's own telling (immersive)

*The one **first-person** file in this folder, kept in Sofia's own voice because for a whole-life persona the lived experience **is** the requirement — "one place that holds my whole life and my work, that I can just talk to." This doubles as the setup story: in a solo world the protagonist is her own resource **and** her own operator, so her telling covers both. The rest of the folder (`customer.md`, `situation-*.md`) is the standard third-person Situation view. This is also the **immersive gold-reference** the `probe-persona` skill points to. **annnä is the app and the agent both.***

## Bio

My name is Sofia. I'm 34, and I teach languages for a living — Spanish and English, mostly one-to-one, some online and some in person. I went freelance three years ago and never looked back, but running my own practice means I'm also my own receptionist, accountant, and the person who has to remember everything.

I'm a tracker by nature. I count my calories, I run and do yoga to stay sane, and I've watched my cycle for years. The trouble is it's all scattered — a nutrition app, Flow for my period, a notes file, and a separate calendar for my students — and none of them talk to each other. What I want is one place that holds my whole life and my work, that I can just talk to instead of tapping through menus.

## Getting started

I set up an account. The first page is empty, and the agent asks whether I want to import anything I already keep elsewhere or start from scratch. I import from Flow — the period tracker I've used for years — and my cycle history and its predictions come onto my board.

## Meals

Now I start interacting with the agent through the console.

> **Me:** I eat breakfast at 8:00.

A form comes up, already partly filled. The title says Breakfast — I could change it, I don't. Start is 8:00; the end is blank, so I put 8:30. For the days there's a row — Sun Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat — with a **Daily** box in front; I click Daily and all seven tick at once, then I unclick Saturday because I skip breakfast on Saturdays, so six days stay checked. (There's a recurrence setting too — weekly, monthly — left on weekly, and I could just type it in words instead.) There's a Buffer field — hovering explains it's time I keep free around something so nothing else books me; I leave it empty. For Location I type "home," and because my home address is already saved it just fills in as "home" — the real address sits behind it, editable in settings, but I don't bother. I confirm; breakfast lands on my board.

> **Me:** Lunch is at 1.

Same form — Lunch, 1:00 pm. I click Daily and leave all seven. Confirm.

> **Me:** Dinner around 7.

Dinner, 7:00 pm, Daily. Done.

## Physical activities

> **Me:** I run Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 6 in the morning, about 45 minutes.

The form shows Run, 6:00, and because I said 45 minutes the end is already 6:45. In the day row I leave Daily unchecked and just tick Mon, Wed, Fri. I use the Buffer this time — 10 before, 15 after — so although my run is 6:00–6:45, I'm actually unavailable 5:50–7:00, and I don't have to say why. Location: the park. Confirm.

> **Me:** Yoga Tuesday and Thursday at 6 in the evening.

Yoga, 6:00 pm, Tue and Thu ticked. Confirm.

> **Me:** Saturday mornings I lift at the gym around 10.

Strength training, 10:00, Sat ticked, location the gym. Confirm. Now my whole week of movement sits on the board beside my meals and my cycle.

## Teaching — sharing my availability

Now I add my work.

> **Me:** I need to share my availability with my students.

A form comes up. It knows this is a lesson; the rest is mine to fill, and some fields it won't let me skip. I title it "Spanish / English lesson." Duration has a minimum and a maximum — I set the minimum to 30 minutes and the maximum to 120, so a student can book anything from a half-hour up to two hours. Buffer, 5 before and 5 after. For the price there are two fields and a cap: I charge a base of **NT$1,000 a month**, then **NT$100 an hour** on top of it, and no student can book more than **10 hours in a month**. I'm not asking the app to take anyone's money — my students pay me the way they always have — but from now on it does the counting for me. For bookable hours I set real start and end times per day — Tue, Wed, Thu 2:00–6:00, and Mon, Wed 6:00–9:00. Mode: online attaches a link — my Zoom, or a Messenger link; in person lets me drop a map pin for where we meet — my place, the student's, or somewhere in between.

Because my meals, runs and yoga are already on my board, students are only ever offered time I'm genuinely free, and they see nothing else of my life.

At the bottom there's a section — **Who is this for?** I enter each student by name and contact: Bobby on WeChat, Millie on Facebook Messenger, Brownie by text. (If I just want a link to send myself, I click **Generate Link** — it copies to my clipboard, a little "Copied" pops up on the button, and I can paste it anywhere.) Then, at the very bottom, I click **Save Template** and submit.

That's when the app sends Bobby, Millie and Brownie each their own booking link, through the app each of them already uses. Every link is unique to that person, so when Bobby books it lands on my calendar as Bobby's lesson — I know exactly who, what, when and where.

## From Bobby's perspective

I'm Bobby. A message from Sofia lands in WeChat: *"Hi Bobby, it's Sofia — please book your English lesson with me, tap here."* I tap it; it opens in my browser.

It welcomes me — *"Welcome, Bobby — book your English lesson with Sofia"* — and shows me a **calendar** of the month. It's not a wall of greyed-out hours; instead each open day shows, right there, *when* she's free — so at a glance I can read her rhythm: **Tuesday to Thursday afternoons** and **Monday and Wednesday evenings**. A few days are blacked out, marked off completely. Nothing about her life shows, only when she can teach, and I can look weeks ahead.

I tap **Monday the 13th** to drill in. Its one open block, **6:00 – 9:00 pm**, opens up.

I want something short. I pick a start of **6:00**, and for the end it only lets me choose from **6:30** onward — 30 minutes is the least Sofia will do, so I can't grab five. I take **6:30**, making it **6:00–6:30**. It asks whether this is one-time or recurring; I choose one-time, pick online, and submit.

A little pop-up: *"Great — can't wait to see you for your English lesson, Monday the 13th, 6:00–6:30 pm."* My confirmation, with the video link, lands back in WeChat.

## From Millie's perspective

I'm Millie. My link comes on Messenger — *"Hi Millie, it's Sofia — book your Spanish lesson here."* I open it to the same calendar and tap **Thursday the 16th**; its afternoon block, **2:00 – 6:00 pm**, opens up. I want **3:00**.

I tap 3:00, choose two hours — **3:00 – 5:00**, the most Sofia allows — online, and hit confirm.

Instead of a confirmation, it tells me *"Sorry, that time was just taken."* When I look, 3:00 is gone from the page. It shows me what's still open, and I take **4:00 – 6:00** instead. That one goes through, and my confirmation lands in Messenger.

## Back to me — the bookings land, and I manage them

Over the next couple of days my week fills in on its own. I don't copy anything over or answer any messages — the lessons just appear on my board, each already labelled, and I get a small note each time one comes in:

- **Monday** evening — Bobby, English, 6:00–6:30, online.
- **Thursday** afternoon — Brownie in person at 3:00, and right after him Millie's Spanish, 4:00–6:00, online.

Because each booking came through that student's own link, I know exactly who I'm seeing, which language, and whether it's online or at my place — without asking anyone. And the lessons sit right there beside my runs, my meals and my cycle: my whole life and my work on one board, finally.

Then something comes up — I'll be away next Monday, so I need to cancel Bobby's lesson. I tell the agent, *"Cancel Bobby's Monday lesson."* It knows this is already our third session together, so it treats it like a standing thing, not a one-off: *"Cancel it, and invite Bobby to pick another time?"* — and it won't message a real person on my behalf without asking first. I say yes. Bobby gets the cancellation in WeChat along with an invitation to rebook, and the slot clears off my board.

A day later Millie can't make her Thursday lesson and moves it herself, straight from her own link. I'm not asked to approve anything — that's her time to give up, not mine to okay. Her old slot frees, the new one drops in, and I just get a note that it changed.

## Bobby rebooks

I'm Bobby. A WeChat message from Sofia: *"Sorry, I have to cancel our Monday lesson — here's a link to grab another time that works for you."* I tap it, and it's the same calendar as before. I pick **Wednesday the 15th**; the evening opens **6:00 – 9:00 pm**, and I take **6:00 – 6:30**, online again. My new confirmation lands back in WeChat.

## And that's the point

Back on my board, Bobby's lesson simply reappears on Wednesday, labelled and ready. I set my life and my rules down once, by talking — and after that the bookings, the cancellations and the reshuffles all happen around the edges of a day that's, at last, all in one place.
