# Situation 5 — the paid-ahead visit and the half-built session

*Third-person, what-happens only. **Provenance: scripted** (added 2026-09-15 under FD-106, re-homed from Situation A — a cancellation credit riding onto a rebooking, and a half-built draft abandoned harmlessly, retold in Debra's world because neither fits Matt's practice). The same board and links as situations 1–3, a few weeks on. Who each player is lives in `story-debra.md`, `customer.md`, and the README cast.*

## A paid-ahead visit is cancelled — and the credit rides onto the next one

Tom pays for his next four Thursday home visits up front — $560, cash, handed over at the door. Debra tells the agent *"Tom paid for four,"* and his thread shows four $140 visits, each already **settled**.

Two weeks later Tom cancels the third of them through his link — a work trip. The visit comes off the board. The $140 he already paid for it is no longer owed for anything, so it does not vanish and it is not handed back: it stays on **his thread as a credit in his favour**, visible to Debra on the board and to Tom on his own page, beside his visits done and paid.

The next Thursday Tom requests a new visit, and the credit **rides onto it**: the new visit arrives already covered, nothing owed, and the credit is gone. No refund machinery runs, because no money ever moved through the app — the cash was in Debra's hand from the start. The *record* is what corrects itself.

## A half-built session is abandoned — and stays harmless

On a Wednesday between patients at Eastside, Debra starts authoring a second session type with the agent — a 30-minute follow-up at $50. It has a title and a length, and the agent is asking its next question — which days and which site should offer it — when Priya arrives for her 3:00. Debra closes the laptop.

Nothing asks her to finish. Nothing finishes for her. The half-made follow-up persists as a **disabled draft**: it publishes nowhere, no patient's link shows a 30-minute follow-up, and no slot on any day is held for it. Priya's page that evening offers exactly what it offered that morning.

Six days later Debra says *"let's finish that follow-up session,"* and the agent reopens it exactly where she stopped — title and length set, the days-and-site question still open. She thinks about it, decides a follow-up is just a shorter treatment session and not worth its own price, and says so. The draft is discarded, and the board is exactly as if she had never started.

## The point

Two quiet corrections, neither of them Debra's to manage. Money paid ahead for a visit that didn't happen stayed the patient's, in plain view on his thread, and settled itself against the next visit he booked — no refund, because nothing ever moved through the app. And a thought she started and walked away from stayed a disabled draft instead of a live surprise on anyone's link, waited for her exactly as she left it, and left no trace when she threw it out. Starting to say something to annnä never commits her to it.
