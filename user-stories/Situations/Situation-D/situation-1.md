# Situation 1 — the perfect case

*Third-person, what-happens only. One week where Debra's whole practice — two office sites, a home-visit round, cash, and her own life — runs on one board that knows the map. The centerpiece is the **compaction**: a cancellation becomes a question, a checked proposal, a patient's yes, and a reclaimed afternoon.*

## The week books itself, in the right places

Debra's board holds her life (the 6:30 swim M/W/F, lunch, Wednesday book club) and her places (Eastside Mon/Wed, Riverside Tue/Fri, home visits Thursday). Her patients book through their own links:

- **Priya** takes Wednesday 3:00 — her link, opened on a Wednesday, offers **the Eastside room**, because that's where Debra is on Wednesdays. The booking lands labelled *Priya · ankle · Eastside*.
- **Mrs. Ito's daughter** books Tuesday 9:30 **at Riverside** — the only shape her link has ever offered, because "Tuesday mornings only" was told to Debra once and stored.
- **Nathan**, new, arrives through the link Priya shared, reads the terms ($90, cash, 45 minutes), and takes Wednesday 10:00 at Eastside.
- **Tom** confirms his address and requests a Thursday home visit; the time that comes back — **2:30** — is one the route can actually hold.

Nothing is offered anywhere Debra won't be, and nothing is offered that her swim, her lunch, or a drive already owns.

## Thursday assembles as a route

By Wednesday night, Thursday reads: **Harold 9:00** (Maple Street) → drive → **Mrs. Gable 11:00** (Oak Hill) → drive → **Tom 2:30** (south side). The drives are real blocks, computed from the stored addresses; the one hand-made number is Debra's own rule — the bridge at rush hour is 20 minutes — which beats the computed value where it applies.

## The cancellation becomes a question

7:40 a.m. Thursday: Mrs. Gable's son cancels the 11:00 through her link — two taps, no phone call. Debra's board updates, and the agent asks **one question**: *"Your Thursday opened up in the middle. Pull the day tighter — toward the morning, or toward the evening?"*

Debra: **"Morning."**

The agent answers with a **proposal it has already checked**: move Tom 2:30 → **10:30** — Debra leaves Harold's at 10:00, the drive is ~25 minutes, Tom carries no constraint against it. **Harold is not in the proposal** — 9:00, will-never-move, not suggested. Nothing has been sent yet: the agent asks *"Want me to ask him?"*, because messaging Tom is an outward act.

Debra says yes → Tom's link carries the question → Tom confirms in two minutes → the board redraws: **Harold 9:00 → drive → Tom 10:30 → home by noon.** Until Tom's yes, his 2:30 stood; the move was never his notice, always his choice. Mrs. Gable's cancelled visit credits off her thread — no money moved, so the record just corrects itself.

One decision remains, and it isn't the agent's to make: the afternoon the compaction just emptied. *"Keep Thursday afternoon blocked for you, or open it for booking?"* Debra: **"Mine."** The links stop offering it — the time she won back cannot be booked out from under her.

Debra's afternoon holds nothing. That was the point.

## The rebook lands where the route can hold it

Later that week Mrs. Gable's son requests **next Thursday**. The times her page offers already contain the drives: nothing before **10:30** (Harold's standing 9:00 ends at 10:00, and Oak Hill is 25 minutes from Maple Street), and nothing that would strand Debra south of an appointment she can't reach. He takes **11:00** — the same slot as always, because the route holds it. A new booking placed itself into a feasible day, and Debra checked nothing.

## The cash, counted

Each completed visit writes its line: Harold $140, Tom $140, Priya $90, Nathan $90, Mrs. Ito $90. Debra says "Harold paid" with the cash in hand and the entry marks **settled**. Friday evening she asks the board what the week made and gets the number — paid, owed, by whom — that used to cost her an hour and a calculator.

## The rest of the business, on the same board

Her own tasks ride the same week: *ask Mrs. Gable's son for a review when she rebooks* (fires Friday), *flyers at the Riverside noticeboard* (an afternoon block next week). annnä supplies no review machinery and no marketing — the tasks are simply **hers**, held like the swim.

## The point

One board, one week: bookings that land at the right site on the right day, a home-visit round with real drives on it, constraints owned by patients honored without discussion, and a cancellation transformed — by one question, one checked proposal, and one patient's yes — into an afternoon of her own. She copied nothing, phoned nobody, and did the route math never.
