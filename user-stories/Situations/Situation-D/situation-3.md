# Situation 3 — the edges, held honestly

*Third-person, what-happens only. The **same board and links**, stress-tested from the outside — one pointed case at a time. Split into what **must work** and what **must be refused / handled**. Who each player is lives in `story-debra.md`, `customer.md`, and the README cast. These runs are **predictions to verify** — the folder exists to test whether annnä's general primitives absorb location, not to claim they already do.*

## Must work

**A move is a proposal until the patient says yes.** The Thursday compaction proposes Tom 2:30 → 10:30. Until Tom confirms through his link, **his 2:30 stands** — the board shows the proposal pending, not the move made. When a different patient once says no, the day simply stays as it was; a declined move changes nothing and blames nobody.

**The direction is Debra's, each time.** "Pull the day tighter" is not a standing policy — the agent asks *which way* ("toward the morning, or the evening?") each time the middle falls out, because the answer depends on the day she wants. Her pick governs that event only.

**Freed time doesn't silently re-open.** The compaction empties Thursday afternoon — and what that emptiness is *for* is Debra's call, not the system's: *"keep it blocked for you, or open it for booking?"* She says mine, and the links stop offering it. Compacting a day and then re-listing the freed half would undo the very thing she asked for; the time re-opens only by her say-so.

**A declared rule beats a computed value.** The map says the bridge is 12 minutes; Debra's stored rule says rush hour makes it 20. Any proposal touching the bridge at 8:30 a.m. is checked against **her 20**, not the map's 12.

**A cancelled visit corrects the record.** Mrs. Gable's cancelled 11:00 comes off her thread — the $140 never owed, the visit never counted. No refund machinery runs, because no money ever moved; the *record* is what corrects itself.

**The new patient arrives through a shared link — the *right* shared link.** Nathan books through the link Priya handed him — **Debra's public front door, not Priya's own** — terms shown, Wednesday at Eastside, no account, no phone call, the booking his and the manage link his. Finding Nathan was Priya's doing, not the app's; the link is just where the referral becomes a booking. *(The refused twin, clarified 2026-08-22: had Priya forwarded her **own** link instead, whatever Nathan booked on it would attribute to **Priya** — a named link answers as its named person, always, and forwarding it hands over your standing, not a way in. The honest path for a named-only practice is Nathan asking and Debra sending him a link of his own; the front door is what makes "without Debra lifting a finger" possible.)*

**Business admin rides the board.** "Ask Mrs. Gable's son for a review when she rebooks" fires as **Debra's own task** the day she rebooks — annnä holds the reminder; the asking is Debra's, done outside the app.

## Must be refused / handled

**An unreachable slot is never offered.** Harold's 9:00–10:00 sits on Maple Street. A patient across town opening their link for that morning is **not shown 10:00** — the earliest slot offered already contains the drive. Feasibility is enforced at the point of booking, by construction; nobody is booked into a slot Debra cannot physically reach, and nobody has to be called and walked back.

**A protected patient is never in the shuffle.** No compaction proposal, ever, includes **Harold** (will-never-move) or puts **Mrs. Ito** outside Tuesday morning. The agent doesn't propose-and-apologize; the constraint is honored **before** the proposal exists. Debra never sees "move Harold?" as an option she must decline.

**A home visit outside her limits gets an honest no.** A request arrives from an address past the highway — outside the area Debra set. The page says so **at submit**: outside Debra's service area, clearly, immediately. Not a maybe, not a request that dies in silence, not a phone call ending in the same no.

**A race for the last slot yields exactly one winner.** Priya and Nathan both tap the last Friday 4:00 at Riverside within moments. Exactly **one** booking is created; the other gets an honest **"that time was just taken"** and the still-open times — never a silent double-book.

**The agent never messages a patient without Debra's yes.** The compaction needed Tom asked — the agent **stopped and asked Debra first** ("Want me to ask him?"). The same line holds everywhere: a cancellation notice, a move request, and above all the money — when a patient's cash is three visits overdue, the board shows Debra the unpaid thread, but a reminder is a message to a real person, and it goes **nowhere** until she says yes.

**A patient probing the link sees nothing of her world.** However anyone probes — looking weeks ahead, drilling blacked-out days, requesting odd times — the link exposes only **bookable time at the day's site**. The swim, the route, the other patients' addresses, and *why* a slot is missing are never rendered, named, or leaked, even as they do the blocking.

## The point

Every edge holds by construction, not apology: the map's truth is enforced where booking happens, a patient's own constraint outranks the optimizer absolutely, a move is a question owned by the person being moved, freed time re-opens only by Debra's say-so, an out-of-area request dies honestly at submit, a race resolves out loud to one winner, and the two across-the-line acts — speaking to a patient, and anything touching money — stay gated on Debra's explicit yes. The board knows the town; it still never oversteps in it.
