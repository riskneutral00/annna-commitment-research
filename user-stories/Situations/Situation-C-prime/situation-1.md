# Situation 1 — standing the shop up

*Third-person, what-happens only. A fresh Hug Ocean account, the dive-center bundle, and the moment Situation C's world becomes possible — plus the refusal that guards the door.*

## An empty account

Hug Ocean's owner signs up. The board is nothing: no kinds, no rules, no resources. She tells the agent what the shop is — courses, fun dives, a boat, a pool schedule, gear, freelance instructors — and the agent suggests the **dive-center bundle** against that description. (Ruled: the empty account is the story; the harder lived-in install is carried by scenario I4, not told here.)

## The bundle stands the shop up

Install walks the same confirmed-proposal path as any authoring, at shop scale: **multi-resource shapes stood up** — roster slots for instructors and divemasters, the boat, the pool, gear pools, air — **course kinds** ready to expand into ordinary commitments under one order, and **governed rules with blanked operands**: the agency-standard structure ships, the shop's own numbers (capacities, buffers, prices, minimums) are hers to fill, each landing only on her confirm. Nothing arrives pre-decided that was hers to decide.

## Situation C starts here

What the install leaves standing is exactly the setup Situation C's clean run begins from: TingTing's booking fans out across the roster, boat, pool, gear and air **because those boards exist in the installed shapes**. The bundle's whole justification is that Situation C never had to explain where its world came from.

## The refusal at the door

A different bundle — tampered, or simply over-reaching — arrives carrying a rule that is not on the engine's menu, and a data block claiming to import "our regular customers." **Refused whole, at the door, entry named:** no partial install, no silent dropping of the bad parts, no customer list riding in on a template. The door's law is the menu and the format; what the format cannot say, no bundle can smuggle.

## The point

A shop went from nothing to bookable through one conversation and one bundle, deciding every number that was hers to decide — and the same door that let a clean bundle stand a shop up refused a dirty one whole. Template supply is owner-publish — anybody can publish a bundle (FD-82; the authoring-and-publish law's home is `../../../marketplace/SPEC.md §2`), and skins alone stay the admin pack pipeline. The door never trusted supply in either regime, which is the point: an owner-published bundle meets the same whole-bundle refusal a curated one would.
