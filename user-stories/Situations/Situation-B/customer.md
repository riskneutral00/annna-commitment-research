# The customer

*Off-app in every version of this story — no account, no calendar. But unlike the dive customer, this one doesn't just fill a form and wait: **they drive the booking themselves.** Told through **Tom Becker**. Third-person, what-happens only.*

## Who

**Tom Becker**, a German backpacker on holiday in Phuket, staying in Rawai for a week. **Reads/writes English.** Holds a German driving license and an **International Driving Permit** — car-and-scooter class, **not** the motorcycle endorsement. He wants a scooter for the week to get around.

## What he does

Everything through Sunbird's **public link**, on his phone, no account:

- **He pulls a bike.** He opens the link, sees the live fleet with photos and prices, and taps a **Honda Click 125i**. That tap is not a rental — it puts the unit into a **hold**: it's now off the link for everyone else, and a **1-hour clock** starts, shown to him on the screen.
- **He works the checklist** the pull opened, against the clock:
  - **Passport** — photographs and uploads it (the image is stored; the physical book stays in his pocket — Sunbird never takes it).
  - **License** — uploads his license + IDP. For the Click, car-and-scooter class is enough; annnä accepts it.
  - **Terms & conditions** — reads Sunbird's T&C and signs.
  - **Deposit** — places the deposit the policy names (represented as committed — a record, not a payment; annnä never charges it, and any damage settlement happens outside the app, gated on his consent).
- **It confirms.** All four clear with twenty minutes on the clock to spare, so the hold **confirms itself** into a rental (Auto). He gets the pickup address and hours.

## His thread

He fills the checklist once, inside the clock. If he'd dawdled past the hour, the hold would have **released** and the Click gone back on the link — he'd have had to pull again and might have found it gone. He picks the bike up at the counter and pays the week's rate there — cash across the counter, outside the app; Ploy marks the rental **paid**, and his record shows it settled beside the represented deposit. **The confirmation also handed him his own manage link** *(scripted beat, added 2026-08-22 — the walk-up half of the guest-manage law)*: mid-week he thinks about keeping the Click through Monday, opens that link, and sees his rental as his to change — extend if the unit's genuinely free after him, cancel if his plans die — with Ploy informed, never asked. He ends up not needing it; the point is the door was his all along, though he arrived off a public link and no one at Sunbird ever typed his name. He rides it for the week, and **brings it back Saturday night though it's due Sunday** — the early return is his to make; the unit starts its 6-hour buffer from Saturday night. He never sees the buffer, the other travellers pulling off the same link, or which sibling Click he ended up on — only "here's your bike."

**Where he hits a wall (and it's honest):** earlier he'd tapped a **CBR** — one of the two big sport bikes — for a day trip. His license isn't **motorcycle-class**, so at the checklist annnä won't accept it for that unit; and both CBRs need the same class, so there's no fallback in that category: the pull can't confirm, and he's told plainly. He takes a Click instead.

**What he must never see:** which specific unit he was assigned, another traveller losing a race for a scarce bike, the buffer timing, or a deposit being anything other than "held." **What he must always see honestly:** the hold clock ticking, and a clear **"no"** when his license or the fleet can't support what he tapped.
