# Situation 1 — the perfect case

*Third-person, what-happens only. **annnä runs the counter.** Four travellers pull bikes off Sunbird's public link over one busy afternoon; the fleet is on annnä and the customers serve themselves. Every hold clears in time, concurrent pulls each land a free unit, a race resolves silently, and a return re-enters after its buffer — no double-books, no key handed out twice, no call. Who each player is lives in the setup stories (`resource-bike.md`, `operator-shop.md`) and the README cast. This establishes the **ceiling** — what annnä makes possible when the fleet is on it.*

## The afternoon's four pulls

Off the same link, at overlapping times:
- **Tom Becker** (English) — a **Honda Click 125i**, one week (a model with fourteen units — no contention).
- **Priya Nair** (English) — a **Yamaha NMAX 155**, four days (a model with only **two**).
- **Marco Rossi** (English) — a **Yamaha NMAX 155**, three days.
- **Anya Petrova** (English) — a **Yamaha NMAX 155**, two days — pulling at almost the same second as Marco, for the **last** free NMAX.

## It just resolves

**Tom, 14:00.** He opens the link, taps a **Click**. The unit drops into a **hold** — off the link for everyone else, 1-hour clock running. He uploads passport and license (car-and-scooter class, fine for a Click), signs, places the deposit; all four clear by 14:40. The hold **confirms itself** (Auto). Clicks are abundant — thirteen still sit on the link — so nobody is competing with him.

**Priya, 14:20.** She taps an **NMAX** — one of only two. It drops into her hold; she clears the checklist by 14:35; confirmed. **One NMAX left.** She never sees the Clicks or Tom.

**Marco and Anya, 14:31 — the race.** Both tap that **last NMAX** within the same few seconds. annnä opens **exactly one hold** — Marco's tap lands first — and Anya's tap does **not** open a second hold on the same unit. Instead her link, already refreshed to the live fleet, shows that NMAX as gone and offers the next fit silently — a **Honda PCX 160** (three of those free). She taps it, clears her checklist, confirmed. Neither of them ever learns there was a contest; there was simply "a bike" for each.

**All four confirmed, zero calls.** Four holds opened, four checklists cleared, one race resolved to a single winner, and the loser was handed a free sibling without a word — all off one link, none of it touching Ploy.

## A return, silently re-shelved

**Saturday night.** Marco brings his NMAX back a full day early (due Sunday). annnä starts that unit's **6-hour buffer** from the **actual** return — Saturday ~21:00 — so it's unpullable until ~03:00, then re-enters the link on its own. **Sunday morning** a new traveller opens the link and pulls that same NMAX, its buffer long past. Nobody arranged the hand-off; the unit simply cooled down and came back.

## The point

One afternoon, four travellers, a fleet of interchangeable units, a dead-heat race for the last NMAX, and an early return — and Ploy did **nothing**. annnä held each tentative pull honestly on its 1-hour clock, blocked each held unit from everyone else, opened exactly one hold in the race and slid the loser to a free sibling, gated every confirmation on the four-item checklist, and re-shelved the early return after its buffer measured from reality. No paper book, no double-book, no key handed out twice, no "I'll hold it for you" that someone forgets. That's the ceiling — the counter running itself.
