# Resource setup — the divemaster

*A divemaster is **not a separate kind of resource** — it's an instructor with a narrower rating. Same admin-made **instructor template** (`resource-instructor.md`), same setup; the only difference is the rating entered, and the **agency documentation** is what decides what that rating may and may not do. Told once through one person (Aroon). Third-person, what-happens only. **annnä is the app and the agent both.***

## Who (the lens)

**Aroon Saetang**, Thai, **freelance PADI Divemaster**. Reads/writes **Thai + English**; **speaks Mandarin**. He assists on the water and guides fun divers; he teaches no courses.

## Setting up

He signs up, lands on the empty board, and tells the console he wants to set up as a divemaster. annnä fills the page with the **same instructor template** — because a divemaster *is* an instructor-shaped resource. He enters his rating: **PADI Divemaster**, with his number.

**Here's the only thing that's different.** annnä checks the rating against the loaded **agency standards**, and those standards — not annnä, not Aroon — decide what the rating unlocks. For a Divemaster the standards **close** the course-teaching permissions an Instructor would get: he **can't certify** anyone. They **open** only what a Divemaster is allowed to do: **guide certified fun divers**, and **assist an instructor** on a course. So the template shows him no course to author or own; the surface simply never offers it. If PADI's rules said something different — or if the center ran **SSI** or **CMAS**, whose divemaster-equivalent rules differ — the permissions would open differently, because they come straight from whichever agency's documentation is loaded.

The rest is identical to any instructor: he sets his written languages (Thai, English) and **Mandarin as speech-only**, sets acceptance to **Review**, connects his calendar, and states his rate.

## Freelance vs. employed

Same two modes as instructors: Aroon is **freelance** (reachable by any center); others are **employed** and exclusive to one center (e.g. Hug Ocean's own DM, or a Japanese center's). See `resource-instructor.md`.

## Set up

Aroon now exists as a resource: a Divemaster who can be placed to **guide fun divers or be a second pair of hands on a course**, but never to certify — a limit the agency documentation imposed, not a rule hardcoded in the app. A divemaster is placed whenever a group course carries more students than one instructor should have in the water alone.
