# Operator setup — the agent

*How a referral agent sets up on annnä, told through **Alex Walker** at **Phuket Travel**. An agent is a taker like a dive center — but it owns no courses and no resources; it refers a customer to a center and takes a commission. It **depends on the dive center**: an agent can only sell what a center it refers to can actually run. Third-person, what-happens only. **annnä is the app and the agent both.***

## Who

**Alex Walker**, who runs **Phuket Travel** — a referral agent. He books no boats and teaches no courses; he sends customers to centers he trusts and earns a cut. He prefers **Hug Ocean**.

## Setting up

**Signing up.** Alex creates his account through the browser, app language English.

**Landing.** He lands on an empty board and tells the console he wants to set up as a referral agent. annnä already has a generic **agent template**, made by the admin — who you refer to, in what order, your commission, and the link you hand customers — and the page fills with it, console on top.

**Declaring who he refers to.** He declares the **centers he refers to, ranked** — **Hug Ocean first**, then a couple of others as backups for dates or languages Hug can't cover. This is the dependency made explicit: Phuket Travel has no courses or resources of its own, so every ranked entry points at a center whose courses, instructors, boats, and availability are the real inventory behind Alex's sale. The language backups matter — a **German** customer Hug can't serve routes to a German-market center whose *employed* German instructor can teach them.

**His terms.** He sets his **commission** and currencies. The commission is **tracked, never moved**: when a referral confirms, annnä computes his cut and records it as an entry on his board and the center's, marked settled when the center actually pays him outside the app.

**His customer link.** annnä builds Alex a customer-facing intake — the same frozen, no-account guest page a center uses — except that when a customer picks a course and submits, the booking is **handed to the ranked center** to assemble against *its* instructors, boat, pool, and rules. Alex never sees the center's resources directly; he sees only whether the center could take it.

**Set up.** Phuket Travel now exists as an operator: a ranked list of centers, a commission, and a customer link that routes bookings into a center's assembly. Because he owns nothing, his whole setup is a set of pointers at the centers beneath him.

## In the situations

- **Clean:** Alex's customer picks a course; the booking hands to **Hug Ocean**, which assembles it exactly as a walk-in would, and confirms back up. The customer never knows two businesses touched it.
- **Dependency showing:** if Hug is at capacity or can't match the customer's language on those dates, the booking **falls to the next center** on Alex's list. If none can take it, his customer's submit comes back the same honest way a specialty with no instructor does — **nothing available** — because an agent can never promise what no center beneath it can supply.
