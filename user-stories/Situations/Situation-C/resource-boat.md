# Resource setup — the boat

*How a day boat sets up on annnä. Boats are the **one resource whose setup genuinely differs boat to boat**, because each runs a **different weekly route** — so this story is told through three: Hug Ocean's own boat and the two Mandarin Queens (5 and 7), which share an operator but run different rotations. Everything else (capacity, min-pax, on-board air, Auto acceptance) is the same shape. Third-person, what-happens only. **annnä is the app and the agent both.***

## The template

Every boat sets up over the same admin-made **boat template**: it asks for the vessel's **seat capacity**, its **minimum passenger count to run**, whether it carries **on-board air** (a compressor), its acceptance mode (**Auto** — a group headed to a site it's already running that day simply appears on its manifest, which builds itself from the divers' forms), and — the part that actually varies — its **weekly route rotation**: which dive site it runs on which day of the week.

## Two owners, three boats

**M.V. Hug Ocean — owned by a dive center.** Hug Ocean owns its own boat, so it's added *inside* Hug's console (`operator-dive-center.md`) using this same boat form. Capacity ~50, on-board air, min-pax to run. Its **route is simple**: it runs the **Racha** route most days (Racha Yai / Racha Noi), because Hug likes to train Open Water at Racha. It sits under Hug Ocean, first in the center's own boat list.

**Mandarin Queen — a standalone operator with a fleet.** **Kittisak Charoen** owns no dive center; he sells seats. He signs up, lands on the empty board, and adds **two vessels** under one account, each its own resource with its **own rotation**:

- **M.V. Mandarin Queen 5** (70 seats): Mon→Sun runs **Racha · King Cruiser · Phi Phi · Racha · Phi Phi · Racha · Phi Phi**.
- **M.V. Mandarin Queen 7** (90 seats): Mon→Sun runs **Phi Phi · Racha · King Cruiser · Phi Phi · Racha · Phi Phi · Racha**.

Both carry on-board air; both are on Auto. The **only meaningful difference** between them — and between them and the Hug Ocean boat — is the rotation, and that's exactly what makes routing non-trivial: on a given day, *which* boat is even going to the site a course needs. A course finishing Advanced at Phi Phi can only ride a boat whose rotation puts it at Phi Phi that day.

For each vessel Kittisak sets the daily **times** — meet 08:00, depart 08:30, back 17:00 — capacity, and min-pax.

## Set up

Three boats now exist as resources — one owned-by-a-center, two under a standalone operator — each with a route rotation, capacity, min-pax, and on-board air, all on Auto. The route rotation is why a boat is reachable for one course and not another on the same day; capacity and min-pax are why a boat runs or scrubs. The full boat roster (capacities, rotations, first-choice/fallback ordering) is in the README.
