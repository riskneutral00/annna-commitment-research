# Resource setup — the gear (equipment)

*How a rental gear shop sets up on annnä, told once through one person (Ta). Every shop's setup is the same shape over the same admin-made **gear template**; only the inventory numbers differ, so shop A and shop B would read identically. Third-person, what-happens only. **annnä is the app and the agent both.***

## Who (the lens)

**Anong "Ta" Petcharat**, who runs **Scuba Revolution** — an open gear shop, full inventory (ScubaPro / Aqua Lung / Mares). No center, no courses; she rents equipment.

## Setting up

**Signing up.** Ta creates her account through the browser, app language Thai.

**Landing.** She lands on an empty board and tells the console she wants to add her stock. annnä already has a generic **gear template**, made by the admin — a rental inventory broken out **by item type and size** — and the page fills with it, console on top.

**Declaring the inventory.** She fills the grid in **by type and size**: BCDs, regulators, wetsuits by size, **masks in every size**, fins by size, boots — each with a count. The point of the size grid: a pack request is only satisfiable if **every** piece in a diver's sizes is in stock. A shop that leaves the mask row empty can't fill a bag that needs a mask, no matter what else it holds.

**How work reaches her.** She sets acceptance to **Auto** — a pack request in a diver's sizes simply appears, pulled straight from the diver's form; she packs one bag, tags it once, and it's that diver's for the whole course.

## Set up

Scuba Revolution now exists as a resource: a full size-grid inventory on Auto. Ta tailored the admin's gear template to her shop.

## What differs between shops (only the grid)

Every gear shop sets up this way; the difference is just which cells are filled. A shop missing a size can't complete a bag that needs it — that single gap is why one shop is a first choice and another the fallback. **Owned gear** is the same form filled inside a dive center (Hug Ocean adds its own gear this way). The gear roster (who stocks what, first-choice/fallback ordering) is in the README.
