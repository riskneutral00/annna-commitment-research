# The customer

*Off-app in every version of this story — no account, no agent, no calendar, only a form. Told through **Jun Wang**. Third-person, what-happens only.*

## Who

**Jun Wang**, from mainland China (Shenzhen), on holiday in Phuket. **Speaks Mandarin**; **reads/writes Simplified Chinese and some English.** Never dived before — buys the **4-day O+A** on a walk-in.

His Mandarin is why the course has to land on a **Mandarin-speaking instructor**. Note the seam: the app's written messages to him go in **Simplified/English**; the *teaching* is spoken **Mandarin** — so an instructor who only writes Traditional can still teach him, because teaching is spoken.

## What he does

Everything through a **tokenized link**, on his phone, no account:
- **Contact & identity** — passport, his languages, contact, arrival = pickup + hotel, emergency contact.
- **Medical (PADI questionnaire)** — answers honestly about **surgery in the last 12 months**. That "yes" trips a **hard block in the app itself**: he can't reach payment until he uploads a doctor's note (stored to the cold vault — a Glacier-class store, `../../../security/SPEC.md §4`) *and* hands the physical paper in at the desk. No one has to chase it.
- **Equipment sizing** — wetsuit, fins (feeds the gear the shop packs).
- **Waiver** — reads and signs.

## His thread

He fills the form once. The medical block is the one thing that stops the booking cold until he clears it. Days later a message tells him his pickup time and place. He meets one instructor for the first days and, if one is swapped mid-course, a second — normal, as far as he knows. He never learns an instructor was replaced, a pool swapped, a gear shop couldn't fit him, or a boat changed. At the end he's certified.

**What he must never see:** the instructor cascade, the pool/gear/air swaps, the boat change — any resource being anything other than "handled."

*The other customers in the week (Mei-Ling, James's group, Lukas, Yuki, Camille) work the same way — off-app, form only, each in their own language. **A group is one booking, several people:** James's party of three is a single booking, but each friend gets their own form link — medical, waiver, and sizes are per-person — and the group confirms only when every member's form clears. If one friend trips the medical gate — a "yes" on the questionnaire — **his form blocks exactly as Jun Wang's did**, until his doctor's note clears; the other two forms stand complete and waiting, nobody re-fills anything, and the group's booking confirms the moment the last gate lifts. See the roster in the README.*
