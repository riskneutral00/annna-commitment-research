# Situation 1 — Everyone on the app (the clean run)

*Third-person, what-happens only. A **whole week at Hug Ocean**, not one sale: four parties on three different courses running across the same days, drawing on shared resources. Everyone who supplies anything is on annnä, every first choice is available, and annnä places all of it against a live view of the whole week's inventory and routes. **Hug uses its own resources first and only reaches past them when its own can't cover** — its own instructors, boat, pool, and gear, then a reachable freelancer, and for a customer it can't serve at all, a silent referral out through the agent. Nobody is called. Who each player is lives in the setup stories (`resource-*.md`, `operator-*.md`) and the README cast. Situation 2 is the same week run the way the industry runs today.*

## The week's four bookings

TingTing takes four walk-ins over a couple of days; annnä holds them against the same shared resources, **Hug's own first**:

| Party | Language | Course | Who serves it | How annnä resolves it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jun Wang** | Mandarin (zh-Hans) | **O+A — 4 days** | **Wang Fang** (Hug's own) | Hug's own Mandarin instructor, free → used first |
| **James Thompson + 2 friends** | English | **Open Water — 3 days (group of 3)** | **Ploy Srisuk** (Hug's own) + DM **Nok** (Hug's own) | own English instructor + own divemaster for the group |
| **Mei-Ling Chen** | Mandarin (zh-Hant) | **Advanced — 2 days** | **Matthew** (freelance) | Wang Fang, Hug's only own Mandarin instructor, is already on Jun Wang → **own not enough** → top free reachable freelancer |
| **Lukas Weber** | German | **Advanced — 2 days** | **Lena @ Scuba Quest** (via the agent) | Hug reaches no German instructor, own or freelance → **referred out** silently through Phuket Travel to a center that employs one |

## Sign-up

Each customer walks into Hug Ocean, TingTing picks the course they ask for and enters their contact, and annnä sends a form link — WeChat for Jun Wang and Mei-Ling, SMS for the others. Each taps it, a browser opens, they fill out the forms — details, medical questionnaire, gear sizes, emergency contact — in the language the intake offers them. James's booking is **one booking, three people**: each of his friends gets their **own** form link, because medical, waiver, and sizes are per-person — and the group confirms only when **all three** clear. **Jun Wang's questionnaire trips the medical gate** — he marks his past surgery, honestly, and the app stops him at the hard block it always runs: no payment until a doctor's note is uploaded and the physical paper handed in at the desk. He clears it in a day and the block lifts — nobody at Hug chases him, because the gate is Hug's own rule and the app runs it end-to-end. Every other medical answer is "no." Everything clears. None of them touches the app again until their course is over.

## The assembly — own first, then the market, then a referral

annnä has a live sense of the whole market's inventory and the whole week's routes — who's free which day, what's in stock, which sites the boat runs which day — so it doesn't fill one booking's slots and then the next. It sees all four courses and every resource at once and places the whole week in one pass, **Hug's own resources first, top available fit after that**:

- **Instructors — own first, then a freelancer when own runs out.** Jun Wang's O+A wants Mandarin; Hug's **own Wang Fang** writes Chinese and speaks Mandarin and is free, so the O+A lands on her — an internal placement. James's group Open Water, in English, lands on Hug's **own Ploy Srisuk**; three students in the water means a divemaster too, and Hug's **own Nok** takes it. Now Mei-Ling's Advanced *also* needs Mandarin those same days — but Wang Fang, Hug's only own Mandarin instructor, is already on Jun Wang. **Own isn't enough**, so annnä reaches the open market and the top free reachable Mandarin freelancer, **Matthew**, takes it. *(Hug's own instructors are internal: no other center and no agent can pull Wang Fang, Ploy, or Nok off Hug — they only ever appear on Hug's own bookings.)* Each instructor sees the whole job — course, days, times, diver, boat, gear — and taps accept (they're on Review).
- **The one Hug can't serve — a silent referral.** Lukas's Advanced is in **German**, and Hug reaches no German instructor, own or freelance. Rather than fail, annnä routes him through the **agent, Phuket Travel**, to a center that *does* employ one — **Scuba Quest**, whose employed **Lena** takes it. Lukas never sees the hand-off; he asked for a German Advanced course and got one, at the shop that could actually staff it. *(This is the out-of-market referral the fragmented market makes necessary — see `operator-agent.md`.)*
- **The pool (Hug Ocean's own).** Day 1 confined water for Jun Wang and for James's group of three — four divers in the water that morning, well under the pool's capacity. Held automatically, on Hug's own pool.
- **The boats — own first, backup for two distinct reasons: full, or committed elsewhere.** Hug's own **M.V. Hug Ocean** (≈45 seats) runs **Racha** and carries the Open Water and O+A training. Hug uses its own boat **while it has room and isn't already committed to another route** — and on **Day 2** both limits bite at once:
  - **Full (pure capacity).** Hug also has a **40-strong dive-club trip** booked to Racha that day. With that group plus Jun Wang's O+A aboard, the house boat is **at its seat cap** — James's Open Water group of three (with Ploy and Nok, five seats) simply doesn't fit. It's not a route problem; there are no seats. So annnä places the overflow on a route-compatible backup, the small **MV Matchanu** (also Racha, room for five), silently. Own boat first; the backup takes only the seats that didn't fit.
  - **Committed (route).** The same morning, Mei-Ling's Advanced finish needs **Phi Phi (PP)** — but the house boat is full *and* going to Racha, not PP. One boat can't be in two places, so annnä engages the **Mandarin Queen** (already running PP that day, on Auto-accept) to carry her finish.

  Two different reasons to reach past the house boat — **it ran out of seats**, and **it couldn't reach the second site** — both absorbed with no call. This is the exact contention that buries the front desk in Situation 2.
- **Gear (Hug Ocean's own).** Each diver's set is packed in their own sizes, pulled straight from their form, tagged once, theirs for the whole course — from Hug's own stock.
- **Air.** The house boat carries its own compressor, so no outside air is placed on the Racha or PP days; only the pool day's tanks are filled, from Hug's own supply.

Every board shows a job that's already theirs, and every slot reports back confirmed. Hug's three in-house bookings go green on Hug's own boards; Lukas's goes green over at Scuba Quest. Nobody is called.

## Day by day (Hug's own three; Lukas runs in parallel at Scuba Quest)

**Day 1 — the pool and the first Advanced day.** By **08:30** the pool is staged: tanks and the tagged gear bags are at the water. **Wang Fang** runs Jun Wang's confined skills; **Ploy** and **Nok** run James's group of three through theirs — arrivals **09:00**, wrap **~14:00**. Meanwhile the house boat is at the pier — meet **08:00**, depart **08:30** — carrying **Mei-Ling** out to **Racha** for the first day of her Advanced, **Matthew** aboard, back by **17:00**. Each instructor ticks skills and logs dives on their own board.

**Day 2 — Racha and PP, on three decks.** M.V. Hug Ocean runs **Racha** — meet **08:00**, depart **08:30** — full with the 40-strong club trip and **Jun Wang** (O+A, first open-water dives), **Wang Fang** aboard. Bumped by the full house boat, **James's group** rides the small **MV Matchanu** to the same Racha site, **Ploy** and **Nok** with them — three open-water dives each, logged against the Open Water sequence. The same morning, the **Mandarin Queen** runs **PP** with **Mei-Ling** and **Matthew** aboard for her Advanced finish; she certifies, and annnä records the Advanced certification. Three boats, two sites — one bumped for seats, one for route — and nobody phoned anybody. All back by **17:00**.

**Day 3 — Racha (finish Open Water).** Meet **08:00**, depart **08:30**, house boat to **Racha**. James's group does their last Open Water dive and **certifies Open Water** — done, they leave with Ploy and Nok. Jun Wang finishes his Open Water the same day and, Open Water locked, rolls into the first Advanced adventure dives with Wang Fang, up to the day's three-dive cap. Back by **17:00**.

**Day 4 — PP (Jun Wang's Advanced finish).** Meet **08:00**, depart **08:30**, house boat to **Phi Phi** (nothing else needs Racha that day, so Hug's own boat carries him). Jun Wang finishes his Advanced dives at PP and Wang Fang marks the course complete; annnä records the Advanced certification. Back by **17:00**. Jun Wang makes an account, and his logbook — every dive Wang Fang logged — is already sitting in it.

*(Lukas's German Advanced runs its own two days at Scuba Quest with Lena, off Hug's boards entirely — the referral means Hug never had to touch it.)*

## The money — recorded, never moved

No money moves through annnä. Each customer pays at the desk the way they always would — cash, or Hug's own card machine — and TingTing marks the booking **paid**; the mark sits on the record both sides can point to. Meanwhile the week writes Hug's ledger as it runs: what Hug owes **Matthew** at his stated day rate, and the seats on the **Matchanu** and the **Mandarin Queen** — each computed from the placements and sitting as an entry on both parties' boards. (Over at Scuba Quest, the same machinery records **Phuket Travel's commission** on Lukas's referral — the center that got the booking owes the agent its cut.) When Hug settles up, outside the app, each entry is marked **settled**. annnä tracks every baht and moves none of them.

## The point

One week, four bookings, three different courses — and annnä placed all of it in a single pass against the whole week's inventory and routes, **Hug's own first**. Its own Mandarin instructor took the O+A, its own English instructor and own divemaster took the group, and only when a *second* Mandarin course collided with its one own Mandarin instructor did annnä reach the open market for a freelancer (Matthew). The one course Hug genuinely couldn't staff — a German Advanced — it **referred out silently** through the agent to the center that employs a German instructor, and Hug's own internal staff stayed un-poachable throughout. The backup boats were engaged for **two distinct reasons only** — the house boat **ran out of seats** (James's group overflowed to the Matchanu) and **couldn't reach a second site** (the Mandarin Queen took Mei-Ling's PP finish) — never before its own boat was exhausted. Not one phone call between any of them. That's the ceiling. Situation 2 is the floor.
