# Resource setup — the pool

*How a training pool sets up on annnä, told once through one person (Niran). Every pool's setup is the same shape over the same admin-made **pool template**; only the numbers differ (depth, capacity, hours), so pool A and pool B would read identically. The market has several pools — see the roster in the README — this is the one story. Third-person, what-happens only. **annnä is the app and the agent both.***

## Who (the lens)

**Niran Jantarakul**, operator of **Water Pro** — a training pool and nothing else. No courses, no boat, no gear; he rents pool time.

## Setting up

**Signing up.** Niran creates his account through the browser, app language Thai.

**Landing.** He lands on an empty board and tells the console he wants to add his pool. annnä already has a generic **pool template**, made by the admin — the shape of a bookable pool — and the page fills with it, console on top.

**Declaring the pool.** He fills the template in: **max depth** (2.5 m) and its **capacity — a count of divers at once, _not lanes_**. He sets its **operating hours**.

**How work reaches him.** He sets acceptance to **Auto** — there's no yes/no to make, so a confined session that fits simply appears on his schedule; he just keeps the water ready. He uploads his hourly schedule once as his standing availability.

## Set up

Water Pro now exists as a resource: a 2.5 m pool holding a stated number, with hours, on Auto, with a live schedule. Niran tailored the admin's pool template to his pool; from here on, sessions land on him.

## What differs between pools (only the numbers)

Every pool in the market sets up exactly this way — the differences are just the fields:
- **Capacity** decides which bookings it can catch: a small pool (say 6) can't take a group; a large one (say 25) can. That single number is why one pool is a first choice and another a fallback.
- **Owned pools** are the same form, filled *inside* a dive center: Hug Ocean adds its own pool through this identical pool form (`operator-dive-center.md`), and it simply sits under the center, first in Hug's own list.

Roster of pools (capacities, first-choice/fallback ordering) is in the README.
