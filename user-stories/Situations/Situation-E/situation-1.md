# Situation 1 — the predicted ceiling

> **Held-out — read as a prediction.** This is what annnä *would* make possible **if** the four primitives are truly general. It is a **bet**, not a claim that annnä already does this.

*Third-person, what-happens only. **annnä runs the ER board unattended.** Over one shift, cases arrive and are placed by type, a year-ahead bid competition resolves by seniority, and a nurse calls in sick and the board re-solves live to cover — and no human is paged for routine work. Who each player is lives in the setup stories (`resource-room.md`, `resource-staff.md`, `operator-er.md`) and the README cast. This is the ceiling the others are measured against.*

## The board, running itself

The envelope is authored (`operator-er.md`); annnä holds the floor. Through the shift, cases arrive:
- A **child with a fever** → placed in **Peds 1** (type clears the rule, under capacity).
- A **road-accident trauma** → **Trauma 1**.
- An **infectious respiratory** adult → **Isolation** — even though three General Bays sit open, the type routes it past them.
- Three **stable adults** → **General Bays A, B, C**.

Each placement is **autonomous** — annnä matches the case's **type** to an admissible room under capacity and seats it, building the floor's live state as it goes. No human decides any of these; the scheduler's routine job is simply gone.

## A bid resolves, a year out

Independently, the annual **time-off bids** for next year's holiday week come due. **Four staff** bid the same week; the floor's minimum staffing can grant only **two**. annnä resolves by the authored policy — **seniority, then quota** — grants the two most senior, and issues the other two a **decline with a reason** ("granted to more senior bidders; you hold priority next cycle"). Nothing was held while the bids sat pending for a year; the resolution consumes the days only now, for the winners. Two people won a scarce thing and two lost it cleanly, with no meeting and no spreadsheet.

## A sick call, re-solved live

Mid-shift, an **RN calls in sick.** Her absence cascades — two General Bays and the Peds coverage lean on her. annnä **re-solves the live board**: it reassigns a floating RN to the Peds child, shifts an available nurse to cover the two bays, and — because a Resident's placement depended on her supervision — re-seats that supervision under an available Attending, **moving commitments that were already active** to close the gap. The floor stays covered. No 3 a.m. phone tree; the re-solve happens in place.

## The human is never called (this shift)

Everything this shift falls **inside the authored envelope** — routine placements, a policy-resolvable bid contest, a solvable sick-call cascade — so annnä acts throughout and **pages no one.** The director and charge nurse do their clinical work; the *scheduling* runs underneath them.

## The point

One shift — placements by type, a year-ahead competition resolved by seniority with clean declines, and a live re-solve that moved active commitments to cover a sick call — and **no human scheduled anything.** *If* annnä's atoms carry type-match admission, a losing competitive request, an ordering over people, and a live re-solve, this is the ceiling: the hardest coordination problem in reach, run unattended. **Whether they do is precisely what Situations 3 and 4 verify — this run states the bet.**
