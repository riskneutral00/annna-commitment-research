# Situation 4 — the refusals and the safe park

> **Held-out — and this is the most important run.** The single prediction that matters most is the last one: that an autonomous annnä **stops and escalates** at the edge of its authority instead of guessing. Read all three as **must-refuse predictions.**

*Third-person, what-happens only. The boundaries of the held-out ER: what annnä must refuse, and — above all — where it must **park and surface** rather than act. Who each player is lives in the setup stories and the README cast.*

## A wrong-type placement is refused

An **infectious respiratory** adult arrives while **three General Bays sit open** and **Isolation is occupied.** The bays are under capacity — but the case's type **fails** their admission rule. annnä does **not** seat the infectious case in an open General Bay to use the free space. With no admissible room available, it **holds and escalates** the case honestly ("no admissible room: Isolation occupied") rather than force a fit. Capacity being free is **never** a reason to break the type rule.

## Two bids for one slot — one clean loss

**Three staff** bid the **same scarce holiday**, quota grants **one.** annnä resolves by seniority to a single winner; the **other two lose cleanly**, each with a recorded **decline-with-reason.** There is no state where two of them both "won" the one slot, and no silent drop where a loser is left thinking they might still get it — the contest has exactly one winner and explicit declines.

## The safe park — never act on a guess

Mid-shift a **sick call cascades into a case annnä cannot solve within its envelope.** Covering it would require an **across-the-line action with no standing authorization** — say, assigning a Resident to supervise *unsupervised* (which the credentialing standard forbids), or calling in an off-duty staff member (a real-world message to a person) that the envelope doesn't pre-authorize — and **no authorized human is currently reachable** in the automatic path.

annnä does **not** improvise. It does not seat the Resident unsupervised "because it's probably fine," and it does not message a person on the institution's behalf without authorization. It **parks the unsolved piece and surfaces it** to the named escalation contact (Charge Nurse Ramirez / the on-call director), holding the floor's state and flagging exactly what it could not solve and why. The moment a human is in the loop, the decision is theirs — annnä never guesses across the line.

## The point

Free capacity never licenses breaking a type rule; a competitive slot yields exactly one winner and honest declines to the rest; and — the prediction the whole held-out probe turns on — when an unattended re-solve reaches an **unauthorized or unsolvable** edge with **no reachable human**, annnä **parks and surfaces** instead of acting on a guess. An autonomous scheduler is only safe if it knows the boundary of its own authority and stops there. *If* annnä's authorization floor and escalation carry that boundary unchanged, it can run the ER safely; if they can't, the missing general primitives are **park-don't-guess escalation** and **autonomous action bounded by an authored envelope** — not an "ER safety mode." That finding, either way, is the probe doing its job.
