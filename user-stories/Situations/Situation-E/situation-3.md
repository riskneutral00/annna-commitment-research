# Situation 3 — the mechanisms, predicted to work

> **Held-out — every "works" below carries a question mark.** These are the **must-work predictions**, one primitive at a time. If any needs an ER-specific patch, the finding is *which general primitive is missing.*

*Third-person, what-happens only. Each of the held-out primitives, isolated and run on its own, so the bet on each is legible. Who each player is lives in the setup stories and the README cast.*

## Type-match admission

A **feverish child** arrives while **four General Bays sit open** and **Peds 1 is free.** annnä places the child in **Peds 1** — not a General Bay, though bays are open and under capacity — because admission is a **type-match first**: the child's type clears Peds' rule and **fails** the General Bay's ("stable adult"). Capacity never gets consulted for the bays, because the type gate closes them first.
**The bet:** that "admits children, up to 1" is annnä's existing **qualification + capacity**, with the rule gating before the count — no new "room type" field. *(Verify: does placement consult a type rule *before* capacity, using the same machinery that puts a *nitrox-rated* instructor on a boat seat?)*

## A bid resolves by seniority, loser recorded

Two staff, **Dr. Senior** (hired 2015) and **Dr. Junior** (hired 2022), both **bid the same holiday week** a year out; staffing grants **one.** annnä resolves by the authored **seniority ordering**: **Dr. Senior wins**, the week becomes her time off; **Dr. Junior loses**, and the loss is recorded as a **system-issued decline with a reason** ("granted to a more senior bidder"). Neither bid held anything while it sat pending; the win consumes the days only on resolution.
**The bet:** that seniority is annnä's **ordering-over-principals** applied to people, and a bid is a **request whose outcome can be a decline** — both from existing atoms. *(Verify: can a request *lose a contest* and record a decline, without a bespoke "bidding" feature? Can an ordering rank the principals themselves?)*

## A sick call re-solves the live board

An **RN goes out mid-shift.** Her coverage spans two General Bays and a Resident's required supervision — **commitments already active.** annnä **re-solves live**: it moves a floating RN onto the bays and re-seats the Resident's supervision under an available **Attending**, adjusting assignments **that were already in progress** to close the gap, and leaves the floor covered.
**The bet:** that annnä can **re-optimize commitments already active**, not only place new ones. *(Verify: can a solve reach *into* live commitments and move them, or does the model only ever assign the not-yet-started?)*

## The point

Type-match admission places by rule before count; a competitive bid resolves by an ordering over people and records the loser's decline; a live re-solve moves even active commitments to cover a drop. Each is a **prediction that one of annnä's general atoms stretches to the ER unchanged.** Where one does, generality is confirmed out-of-sample; where one doesn't, **name the missing primitive** — do not add an ER feature. Situation 4 tests the other half: what must be **refused**, and where annnä must **stop.**
